Privacy Policy

Beta stage · Last updated: August 15, 2026

Overview

Best Currency AI is currently a decision-support platform under development. This policy explains how we handle the small amount of personal information you share through our early-access waitlist form. It does not cover trading activity, because the platform does not execute, alter, or close trades on your behalf, and we do not collect order, account, or brokerage data.

What we collect

When you join the early-access waitlist, we collect only what you enter in the form:

  • Name — so we can address you personally.
  • Email address — so we can send you early-access communications.
  • Country — for regional segmentation of early access.
  • Forex experience level — to shape onboarding and beta feedback.
  • Biggest challenge when analyzing currencies — to improve the product roadmap.

How we use your information

Your information is used for one purpose only: early-access communications. That means invitations, status updates, and product news related to the waitlist. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Storage and retention

Waitlist data is stored by the form backend service that powers the waitlist form and is retained only as long as needed to manage early access. When early access has concluded or you ask us to delete your data, we remove it.

Your rights

You can request a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, at any time — no questions asked. To do so, email us at the address listed in the contact section below.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact us at the email address published on the landing page.

Beta-stage transparency: Best Currency AI is a development milestone, not a production deployment. We are pre-revenue and pre-production, and we treat your information accordingly — collected only for early-access communication, never sold.
