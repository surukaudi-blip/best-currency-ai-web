Privacy Policy

Beta stage · Last updated: August 18, 2026

Overview

Best Currency AI is currently a decision-support and research-intelligence platform under development. This policy explains how we handle the personal information you choose to share through our structured beta-program application form. The platform does not execute, alter, or close trades, and the beta application does not request brokerage credentials, account numbers, order history, or financial account data.

What we collect

When you apply to the structured beta cohort, we collect only the information you enter in the form:

  • Name — so we can identify and contact applicants.
  • Email address — so we can communicate about beta selection, onboarding, and follow-up research.
  • Country or region — to understand regional representation, with an initial focus on Indonesia and Southeast Asia.
  • Forex experience level — to build a cohort with a useful mix of experience levels.
  • Analysis frequency and primary platform — to understand current research habits.
  • Analysis challenges and beta motivation — to evaluate product fit and improve the product experience.
  • Beta-program consent — confirmation that you agree to be contacted for this research/product-validation program.

How we use your information

Your information is used to manage structured beta recruitment, select and segment the research cohort, communicate beta instructions, and evaluate product usefulness, explanation clarity, workflow comprehension, repeat usage, retention potential, and willingness to pay. Beta feedback may inform UI, onboarding, wording, navigation, and explainability improvements. It is not used to alter the frozen model during Fresh Out-of-Sample validation.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Storage and retention

Beta application data is received and stored through the form backend service used by the website. We retain it only as long as reasonably needed to manage beta recruitment and product research, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights

You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you or ask us to delete it. You may also withdraw from beta communications at any time.

Contact

For privacy questions, access requests, or deletion requests, contact Best Currency AI through the contact channel published with the beta invitation or application communication.

Beta-stage transparency: The structured beta is product validation, not financial advice, a trading-signal service, or evidence of model accuracy. Beta feedback can improve the product experience while the Fresh OOS validation model remains frozen.
