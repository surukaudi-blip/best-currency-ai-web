Shared Intelligence Architecture

Every asset must pass the same explainability and governance pipeline.

01 · MARKET DATA

Canonical signal

Asset-specific price, strength, trend, spread, flow, or fundamental signals. News is never substituted for missing market data.

02 · EVIDENCE

Context + source quality

Macro, filings, inventories, regulation, protocol events and credible journalism are scored by relevance, recency, independence, and source tier.

03 · DECISION

Reasoner + counter-thesis + risk

The AI must show supporting evidence, opposing evidence, invalidation conditions, source gaps, and why a view is promoted or deprioritized.

News & Evidence Integrity

Credibility is a system rule, not a cosmetic publisher badge.

A

Primary / Official

Regulators, central banks, government agencies, exchanges, company IR, official foundations. Highest factual authority for directly relevant events.

B

Licensed Financial Wires

Reuters and Bloomberg through approved licensed access only. No unauthorized scraping or redistribution.

C

Reputable Financial Press

Financial Times, Wall Street Journal, CNBC and comparable publishers provide strong context, but material claims are cross-checked.

D

Discovery Aggregators

News aggregators help discover coverage. They cannot independently confirm a decision or override higher-tier evidence.

SOURCE GOVERNANCE
Fail closed when evidence quality is insufficient.
A single headline cannot confirm a market decision. Conflicting high-quality sources must remain visible as disagreement. Missing licensed data must be labeled unavailable rather than replaced by an unverified substitute. Trade execution remains OFF.
